Privacy Policy

Match Week Food Diary — last updated 2026-08-15

Match Week Food Diary is a private, personal-use application. It is not distributed publicly and is used only by its own account holders.

What data is collected

Food and exercise entries (text, photos, estimated calories), body stats you choose to enter (weight, height, age, activity level), weigh-in history, and, if you connect a WHOOP account, daily calorie burn data from WHOOP's API.

How it's stored

All data is stored in a private database on the app's own hosting infrastructure. It is not sold, shared, or made available to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Third-party services

Photos and text may be sent to Anthropic's API to estimate calorie content. If a WHOOP account is connected, calorie burn data is retrieved from WHOOP's API using OAuth authorization you explicitly grant, and can be revoked at any time from WHOOP's own account settings or by disconnecting within this app.

Data deletion

Account holders can delete individual entries at any time within the app. For full account or data deletion requests, contact the app owner directly.
