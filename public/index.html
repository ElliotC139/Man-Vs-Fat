      Apple Health and Android's Health Connect are native apps — no website can read them directly, whatever it promises. What does work is the export file they already make.

      Where to find it

      iPhone: Health → your photo, top right → Export All Health Data. Share the zip to Files, then pick it here.

      Android, or a smart scale: any CSV with a date column and a weight column — Health Connect exporters, Withings, Garmin and most scale apps all produce one.

      Account

      Change password

      The camera is only ever used for scanning a barcode, and only while the scanner is open. This app tells your browser that nothing else loaded inside it — no script, no embedded page — may reach the camera at all.

      To allow it for this site alone, rather than for every site you visit: on iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap aA in the address bar, choose Website Settings, and set Camera to Allow. That grant applies to this address and nothing else. On Android Chrome it's the padlock in the address bar → Permissions → Camera.

      Better still, add this to your home screen: an installed app keeps its own permissions between launches, so it asks once rather than every session. A run of scans only asks once either way — the camera is held open for a couple of minutes between them.
